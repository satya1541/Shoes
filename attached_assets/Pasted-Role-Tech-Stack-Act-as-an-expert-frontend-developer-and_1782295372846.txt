Role & Tech Stack:
Act as an expert frontend developer and creative technologist. Build a highly immersive, single-page scroll-driven landing page for a premium sneaker brand ("VYPR"). Use React, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion. The entire website must feel like a seamless cinematic experience where scrolling triggers bold visual transitions. Use Lucide React for UI icons.

Overall Aesthetic & Typography:

Typography: Ultra-bold, massive compressed sans-serif fonts (like Impact or Anton) that stretch across the screen. Text elements should feel tightly integrated with the product imagery.

Visual Style: High-contrast, sleek, and premium. The products must look like they are floating with subtle dynamic depth shadows to create a 3D effect.

Theme/Tone: The site primarily defaults to a dark, high-energy aesthetic with vibrant color-blocked sections. However, include a toggle for a clean, professional light mode. In light mode, ensure the typography remains razor-sharp and dark against minimalist backgrounds, keeping the layout sophisticated.

Animations & Mechanics:

Use Framer Motion's useScroll and useTransform to tie the background color changes, text fades, and image scaling directly to the user's scroll position.

Ensure all background transitions feel fluid, not jarring.

Hovering over buttons (like "SHOP" or "NOTIFY ME") should instantly invert their colors.
